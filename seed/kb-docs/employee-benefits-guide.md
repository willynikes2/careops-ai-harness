This guide explains the benefits available to full-time CareOps employees in the synthetic demonstration workplace. Use it when reviewing enrollment choices or checking a benefit with the people team. Benefit questions should include the relevant section of this guide, without personal health details. The guide describes coverage; it does not display enrollment or replace an individual coverage confirmation.

## §1 Medical

CareOps offers two medical plans, **Core PPO** and **Plus PPO**. Review the enrollment materials for the differences in employee contributions, deductibles, and covered services before choosing a plan. The people team can help locate those materials and explain the enrollment process. Do not assume that a plan name confirms your current enrollment or that every service is covered under both options.

## §2 Dental

Dental cleanings twice a year are covered at **100%**. When scheduling, tell the dental office which plan you have and ask it to confirm coverage. Other dental services may have different terms; this cleaning benefit should not be used as a promise that all dental work is fully covered.

**Claims.** This guide does not describe how dental or vision claims are filed, and it does not say whether the office or the employee submits them. For claim questions, contact the people team, who will explain the process for your plan.

## §3 Vision

The vision benefit includes an exam every **12 months**. Check the date of your last covered exam before booking the next one. Ask the people team for enrollment information if you cannot find your coverage details. This section does not specify an allowance for frames, lenses, or contact lenses.

## §4 Retirement

CareOps offers a **401(k) with a 4% employer match after 90 days**. Employees can review their contribution elections through the enrollment process. Ask the people team to confirm your eligibility date and explain how to update an election before making a change.

## §5 Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program provides **6 free counseling sessions per year**. Employees can ask the people team how to access the program. There is no need to describe a personal concern in an operations chat or attach counseling information to a PTO request. Keep benefit-access questions separate from sensitive personal details.
