This policy covers paid time off for full-time CareOps staff in the synthetic demonstration workplace. Employees should review their current balance and coordinate coverage with their manager before requesting time away. CareOps holds the current request status and balance; a conversation about time off is not an approved absence.

## §1 Accrual

Full-time staff accrue **6.67 hours per month**, representing **80 hours per year**. The monthly figure is rounded for display. Use the available hours shown in CareOps when planning a request rather than calculating an assumed balance from tenure. Accrual information describes the policy and is not a substitute for the employee's current balance record.

## §2 Requests

Requests are submitted through **CareOps**, require **manager approval**, and need at least **2 business days** notice. Choose the intended calendar date before submitting. If a phrase such as “next Friday” could mean two dates, confirm which date you mean. Discuss coverage with your manager, then check the request status in CareOps. A submitted request remains pending until the manager records a decision.

## §3 Balance

A request cannot exceed available hours. **No negative balances** are allowed. Review both available and pending hours before sending another request, since an existing pending request may already reserve time. If the displayed balance is unexpected, ask the people team to review the record before trying to submit a different amount.

## §4 A full day of PTO

A full day of PTO is **8 hours**. The “Request a day” form uses this amount. Check the selected date and the requested hours in the resulting record. A request for one day does not automatically include neighboring workdays or a whole calendar week.

## §5 Carryover

Unused PTO carries over up to **40 hours**. Employees should review remaining time with their manager before the year ends and make a practical coverage plan. Carryover is a limit on unused hours moved into the following year, not an additional award of hours. Ask the people team about any record that appears inconsistent with this policy.
