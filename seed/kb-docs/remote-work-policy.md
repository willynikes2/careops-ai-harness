CareOps uses a hybrid arrangement for eligible office work in this synthetic demonstration workplace. The purpose is to support focused work while keeping service coverage predictable. A remote day is a working day, not paid time off. Employees should agree on availability, handoffs, and any necessary office attendance with their manager before changing their regular schedule.

## §1 Office staff

Office staff may work remotely up to **2 days per week with manager agreement**. Discuss the proposed days with your manager and check that shared duties remain covered. Manager agreement applies to the working arrangement; employees should not assume that an informal conversation establishes a permanent schedule.

Stay reachable during agreed working hours and keep shared work records current. If an office meeting or coverage need affects an agreed remote day, coordinate the change with your manager. Do not treat unused remote days as a bank of days that can be added to a later week. When requesting PTO for an agreed remote day, follow the PTO policy and check the resulting request status.

## §2 Clinical field staff

Clinical field staff are **not eligible** for the remote-work arrangement. Their duties depend on field coverage and the assigned service schedule. A day spent completing documentation does not automatically change a clinical field role into an eligible office role.

Questions about role classification should go to the manager and people team. A request for a different work arrangement does not change the employee's responsibilities until it has been reviewed through the appropriate workplace process.

## §3 Devices

Devices must use the **company VPN** when working remotely. Check the connection before opening company resources, and contact the support team if it cannot be established. Do not work around a connection problem by copying work material into a personal account.

Use a workspace where screens and conversations are not exposed to others. Lock the device when stepping away. Report a lost device or unexpected access promptly through the normal support process, and avoid placing passwords or sensitive details in an operations chat.
