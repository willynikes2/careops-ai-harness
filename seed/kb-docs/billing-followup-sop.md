This procedure explains how CareOps billing staff record the work needed to move an assigned claim forward. It applies to the synthetic demonstration workspace. A follow-up is a work record attached to a claim, not a message sent to a payer and not evidence that a payer has accepted a correction. Check the claim identifier before adding a task.

## §1 Task kinds

Use one of four task kinds: **AUTH_DOCUMENTATION**, **CODING_REVIEW**, **PAYER_CALL**, or **APPEAL_PREP**.

Use **AUTH_DOCUMENTATION** to obtain or review authorization records, including work prompted by a CO-197 denial. Use **CODING_REVIEW** when the denial or claim record calls for review of coding information. Use **PAYER_CALL** when a payer clarification is the next step. Use **APPEAL_PREP** to gather the records and explanation needed for an appeal.

Choose the kind that describes the next piece of work. Do not use a task kind as a substitute for reading the denial reason. If more than one type of work is needed, keep each note clear about the action it describes and check existing tasks to avoid recording the same work twice.

## §2 Notes

Every follow-up needs a **one-sentence note**. State a concrete action and the evidence or question involved. For example: “Obtain the authorization record for this claim and review the retro-authorization deadline.” The note should be useful to someone returning to the claim later.

Do not put passwords or unrelated personal details in a task note. Avoid vague wording such as “handle claim” because it does not explain what remains to be done. Read the note before submitting and check that it belongs to the selected claim.

## §3 Due dates

Follow-ups are due within **5 business days**. Review open tasks regularly so a handoff or staff absence does not leave work unattended. Payer filing and response deadlines still apply and may require earlier action.

When reviewing progress, distinguish a task that has been recorded from work that has been completed. A newly created follow-up does not automatically change the claim to resubmitted, appealed, or paid. Any claim status change must follow the permitted billing workflow.
